Project: Aurelia Enterprise Cloud Data & Analytics Platform

Business problem: Aurelia's CRM, project operations, resource management, finance, and reporting data are fragmented across separate systems. Management relies heavily on manual reconciliation and lacks a trusted, integrated view of sales, projects, resources, financial performance, and profitability.

Project objective: Design and implement a modern cloud data and analytics platform that integrates Aurelia's operational systems into governed analytical models and makes trusted business information available through Power BI, APIs, automation workflows, and eventually an AI business assistant.

Primary business outcomes: Reduce manual data integration, establish trusted business metrics, improve project profitability visibility, improve capacity/utilization reporting, provide sales-to-revenue traceability, automate selected business processes, and establish scalable data-engineering practices.
