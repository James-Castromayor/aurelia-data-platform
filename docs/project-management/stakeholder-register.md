# Aurelia Stakeholder Register

## Purpose

This document identifies the key fictional stakeholders for the Aurelia Enterprise Cloud Data & Analytics Platform and their primary interests in the project.

| Stakeholder / Group   | Role                             | Primary Interest                                                  | Influence |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | --------- |
| Executive Leadership  | Project Sponsor / Steering Group | Revenue, profitability, growth, utilization, business performance | High      |
| Sales Director        | Business Owner — Sales           | Pipeline, win rate, forecasting, CRM data quality                 | High      |
| Finance Director      | Business Owner — Finance         | Revenue, costs, AR, AP, budgets, profitability                    | High      |
| Operations Director   | Business Owner — Operations      | Project delivery, utilization, capacity, operational efficiency   | High      |
| Project Managers      | Business Users                   | Budgets, actuals, milestones, staffing, project status            | Medium    |
| Resource Managers     | Business Users                   | Capacity, allocations, utilization, future demand                 | Medium    |
| Sales Team            | Business Users                   | Leads, opportunities, customers, sales pipeline                   | Medium    |
| Finance Team          | Business Users                   | Invoices, payments, costs, financial reconciliation               | Medium    |
| Consultants           | Operational Users                | Project assignments and timesheets                                | Low       |
| Data & Analytics Team | Technical Owner                  | Data pipelines, models, quality, analytics and governance         | High      |
| Application Team      | Technical Stakeholder            | Operations application and APIs                                   | Medium    |
| IT / Security         | Governance Stakeholder           | Security, access, reliability and compliance                      | High      |

## Stakeholder Management Principle

Requirements should be validated with the business owner responsible for the affected domain.

Technical implementation decisions remain the responsibility of the engineering and architecture functions, while business stakeholders define the required outcomes.
